# Technology Workforce & Layoff Intelligence (2020–2023)

**Business Intelligence | Workforce Analytics | Microsoft Power BI**

## Project Overview

This project is a Business Intelligence and Workforce Analytics case study examining technology-sector layoffs between 2020 and 2023.

Using Microsoft Power BI, the project analyses workforce reduction patterns across 1,642 companies, 29 industry categories and 54 countries, covering approximately 389,330 reported employee layoffs.

The objective is to transform workforce data into actionable analytical insights by examining geographical concentrations, industry exposure, temporal patterns, company-level layoff distribution and company funding stages.

The interactive dashboard provides multiple perspectives on workforce disruption, allowing users to explore the data through geographical, industrial, temporal and organisational dimensions.

## Key Analytical Findings

### 1. Geographical Concentration of Affected Companies

The San Francisco Bay Area records the highest number of affected companies, with approximately 580 organisations, followed by New York City and Boston.

**Insight:** Layoff activity is geographically concentrated within major business and technology hubs in the dataset, highlighting the importance of regional workforce analysis.

### 2. Industry Exposure and Workforce Impact

Finance has the highest number of affected companies, followed by Healthcare and Retail. However, the employee layoff distribution differs across industries, with particularly prominent totals in Retail, Consumer and Transportation.

**Insight:** The number of companies experiencing layoffs does not necessarily correspond to the total number of employees affected. Assessing both measures provides a more comprehensive understanding of industry-level workforce disruption.

### 3. Temporal Patterns in Workforce Reductions

The monthly analysis reveals significant fluctuations in layoff activity. January 2023 records the most prominent visible monthly peak, at approximately 85,000 layoffs, while November 2022 also shows a substantial increase of approximately 50,000.

**Insight:** Workforce reductions were concentrated within particular periods rather than being evenly distributed across the reporting timeline.

### 4. Workforce Reductions Across Company Stages

Post-IPO companies record the highest displayed number of affected organisations and the largest employee layoff total among the company stages represented. Series B and Series C follow in terms of affected company counts.

**Insight:** Company funding and development stages offer another useful dimension for understanding the distribution of workforce reductions and investigating organisational vulnerability.

### 5. Company-Level Layoff Distribution

The company-level percentage visual identifies Philips, Meta and Salesforce as the three largest displayed segments, accounting for 30.08%, 21.72% and 21.47%, respectively.

**Insight:** Company-level analysis helps identify the organisations with the largest represented shares of the chart's percentage measure, supporting more detailed organisational comparisons.

*Note: The percentage figures represent the dashboard's displayed distribution. They should not be interpreted as individual employee reduction rates without verifying the underlying calculation.*

## Business Intelligence Value

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence can support workforce and organisational analysis by:

- Identifying geographical concentrations of workforce disruption.
- Comparing the scale of layoffs across different industries.
- Monitoring temporal changes in workforce reduction activity.
- Examining workforce impact across company funding and development stages.
- Comparing company-level layoff distributions.
- Supporting evidence-based investigation of workforce and organisational trends.

## Project Scope and Limitations

This is a descriptive and exploratory analysis based on the available dataset and dashboard measures. The findings identify patterns and distributions but do not establish the causes of layoffs or demonstrate causal relationships between funding, company maturity and workforce reductions.

All reported figures reflect the dashboard's displayed metrics and should be interpreted within the scope of the underlying dataset.
